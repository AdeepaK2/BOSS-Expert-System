Business Opportunity Screening System

A first-pass screening for a proposed small business. It applies 25 decision rules captured from a small-business advisor to what you tell it, and returns a recommendation you can trace back to the rules that produced it.

Its real output is not the verdict. It is the list of things you have assumed but not yet checked.

Seven short sections, about five minutes. Nothing you enter leaves this device.
